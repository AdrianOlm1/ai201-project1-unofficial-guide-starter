University of California - Santa Cruz Reviews

At the University of California - Santa Cruz (UCSC), students frequently mention the stunning natural environment , with the campus set in a picturesque redwood forest that enhances the academic experience. Many reviews highlight the supportive professors and engaging classes , especially in prevalent majors like STEM and social sciences. However, recurrent concerns relate to the housing crisis , as both on-campus and off-campus options are limited and pricey, leading to significant stress for many students. The social scene appears quieter, with some feeling isolated due to the campus layout and limited nightlife , while others appreciate the opportunity to find their niche in smaller communities. The university's emphasis on diversity, sustainability, and social justice is appreciated, though some students feel that the administrative response to these values, especially regarding student welfare and support services, could improve. Overall, UCSC is viewed as a unique institution that excels academically for those aligned with its environmental and progressive ethos but presents challenges in housing and social engagement.
